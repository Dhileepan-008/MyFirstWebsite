HTML5: Media Elements

HTML5 provides built-in audio and video elements.

Audio

<audio controls>
  <source src="audio.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>

Video

<video controls width="500">
  <source src="video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

Useful attributes: controls, autoplay, muted, loop, poster.

Exercise: Create a "Cybersecurity Awareness" page with one audio clip and one video. Try controls, muted and loop.
